    How I think about business

    Where the counter meets the dashboard

    A neighbourhood shop already has trust, credit relationships and local knowledge. Going online adds reach, and every sale leaves a record. Data science turns those records into decisions.

    Traditional business

    Face-to-face trust, udhaar (credit) relationships, knowing which customer wants what before they ask.

    +

    E-business

    Orders at any hour, customers beyond the street, digital payments, and a clean data trail for every sale.

    +

    Modern technique

    Demand forecasting, customer segmentation and automation that turn records into the next stock order.

    The products I want to build live in the overlap. Small tools a shop owner can trust, backed by models that explain themselves.
